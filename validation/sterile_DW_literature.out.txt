Interpretation guide:
  - Agreement at 10-20% relative (|Δ/H| < 0.2) is the validation target;
    Hannestad's own numerical conventions differ from PRyMordial at that
    level, so tighter is a pleasant surprise.
  - Absolute difference |Δ| > 0.1 on the fully-thermalised point (A) would
    suggest a thermodynamics-baseline bug, not a sterile-production one.
  - The low-thermalisation point (C) has small absolute values so relative
    differences are easier to inflate; report |Δ (abs)| too.
